# Động từ & Danh từ
## 1. Xu hướng tăng
Instead of the verb "increase", we also have:
- to Rise -> a rise 
	→ The number of students **rose** significantly in 2020.  
	→ There was a sharp **rise** in population.

- to Climb -> a climb
	→ Sales **climbed** steadily over the year.  
	→ The chart shows a gradual **climb** in profits.

- to Grow -> a growth
	→ The economy **grew** rapidly after 2015.  
	→ There was strong **growth** in tourism.

- to Show an upward trend
	→ The data **shows an upward trend** from 2010 to 2020.
## 2. Xu hướng giảm:
Instead of "decrease", we also have
- to fall -> a fall
	→ The price **fell** sharply in 2018.  
	→ There was a dramatic **fall** in demand.
	
- to drop -> a drop
	→ The number of visitors **dropped** suddenly.

- to reduce -> a reduction
	→ There was a significant **reduction** in costs.

- to show a downward trend
## 3. Sự biến động:
- to fluctuate -> a fluctuation
	→ The temperature **fluctuated** throughout the day.  
	→ There was a slight **fluctuation** in prices.

- to vary -> a variation / volatility
	→The results **varied** between different groups.
	→ It can be seen to have a **variation / valocity** between different groups.
