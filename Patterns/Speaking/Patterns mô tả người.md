
- **Pattern quan trọng:**
    
    - **To + possessive + noun:**  
        → To her amusement
        
    - **feel + adj:**  
        → feel comfortable
        
    - **require + noun:**  
        → requires patience
        
    - **a + adj + noun:**  
        → a reliable man
        
    - **such a + noun (person):**  
        → such a pessimist
        
    - **express + noun:**  
        → express optimism
        
    - **dis + noun = nghĩa phủ định:**  
        → disinterest
        
    - **one’s + noun:**  
        → anyone’s enthusiasm
        
    - **as + a + noun (person type):**  
        → as an introvert
        
    - **sympathize with + someone:**  
        → sympathize with her
        
    - **a woman of + noun:**  
        → a woman of great wisdom
